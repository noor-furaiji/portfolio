About

Sales and marketing, in three languages.

I help companies sell across languages and borders. After studying English translation, I worked as an interpreter and project team lead at the Iraqi Red Crescent. At Hanwha E&C's site in Iraq I handled logistics and procurement, negotiating with suppliers alongside Korean colleagues. That experience, and the growing trade between Korea and the Middle East, brought me to Korea: Korean at Yonsei, then an MA in International Business at Ewha.

At KARABA I learned that overseas buyers have to trust the company before they trust the salesperson. So I made that trust visible: SNS content showing our real work, targeted ads, a web-based quotation system and digital materials for buyers. My goal is to keep doing both: understand new markets and customers, and grow businesses abroad with sales and marketing working together.
